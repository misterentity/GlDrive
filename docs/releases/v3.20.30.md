# GL Drive v3.20.30

The Upcoming tab no longer cuts off its content. The two column grids clipped dates and episode names and collapsed movie titles to two letters; they are replaced by rows with the poster, the full title, episode, network, genres and rating. Long titles wrap instead of being trimmed.

The detail panel shows a larger poster, air date, time and network for a show or release dates for a movie, and the whole synopsis with a scrollbar instead of an ellipsis after three lines.
