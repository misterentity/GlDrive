**v3.15.2 was built, tested, published through the standard pipeline, installed and verified healthy on this Windows installation during the bounded production monitor, with the known external SYN FTP outage recorded separately.** Final release commit is `6538b6cbd29c50b6b0de0eae7595dd59747d266b`; exact-commit CI 40741978674 passed. ## Initial audit and resolved causes

The audit captured all 24 retained application logs at 2026-10-28 17:03:00 UTC. They span 2026-10-26 00:00:00.851 through 2026-10-28 10:02:23.936 local (UTC−07:00): **2,487,110 records, 147 warnings, zero errors and zero fatals**. The initial installed v3.15.0 portion contained 13,697 records and two warnings. Every initial warning was classified; absence of Error/Fatal records did not establish functional health.

The work corrected these underlying causes:

- **Remount scheduling:** historical SYN activity made 31 background mount attempts in about 52 seconds while local account admission held new network logins. An expired health deadline bypassed bounded retry timing and produced negative delays. The fix retains independent account/authentication/backoff holds and derives optional supervisor/probe deadlines from current health state. Health/probe toggles, recovery, endpoint changes and shared-account aliases cannot retain obsolete supervisor deadlines. Local admission denial does not invent a remote outage; retry counters saturate safely.
- **Timed settings ownership:** changing a leased value away and back before periodic reconciliation allowed the older lease to overwrite the newer choice at expiry. Durable typed/raw/automation settings saves now relinquish ownership immediately, preserving the decision across restart. Unrelated saves, failed saves and the lease's own writes retain correct ownership. Shutdown removes the observer.
- **Notification configuration:** Zephyr's watch path `/recent` had no categories. The validated settings path changed only that server's notification watch path to `/`, requiring no FTP remount or IRC restart. Monitoring restarted at 17:09:01.313 UTC and seeded eight categories at 17:10:03.811 UTC. This was an operational configuration repair, not a changed application default.
- **Source-hint discovery, identified during v3.15.1 monitoring:** a pathless queued source hint was treated as proof of a synthetic section path, bypassing configured/watch-path discovery. Six resumed requests failed with no activity even though the inspected source release existed at its watch-path location with 23 entries. The v3.15.2 correction treats hints as candidates: preferred path first, then ordinary configured/watch alternatives, with real CWD existence checks before pinning. Active hints use bounded candidates and a linked probe deadline at the existing scan cadence; network completion is followed by current-hint, eligibility and ownership checks. Late hints arriving during initial FTP awaits are validated before declaring no source; unavailable or failed hint probes produce an inconclusive verdict. Existing partial/destination paths remain pinned.

Other initial findings were external or recovered: SYN TCP unavailability, PreDB 503s/timeouts, tracker/metadata failures, one provider 429, recovered BNC/main-pool timeouts, source relocation/deletion, transient FXP resets and historical nuke entries. Twelve of 13 failed FXP operations had later race-completion/already-present evidence; the remaining prior-update interruption retained queued work/ownership. Two earlier interrupted races were queued with one resume each; their completion is not claimed without later evidence. Debug review found expected SFV cancellation/probes and one direct transfer route that recovered through relay. No live native TLS crash signature was found.
