# GL Drive v3.11.22 — finished races no longer park as "waiting for files"

## Fixes

- A race whose files were all present on every site could return its slot as
  "Waiting for remaining release files" and sit queued for ~40 minutes. The
  30-second SFV refresh revoked the already-parsed manifest before re-reading it,
  so a failed re-read (a 15 s borrow wait behind a busy transfer, or the account
  login cap) left the race with an "unknown" manifest at the moment it finished.
  An aged manifest is now refreshed without being revoked; a manifest whose size
  changed is still treated as unknown until re-read.
- A race no longer parks on listings that started before its latest delivery. A
  zipscript stub or progress marker in such a listing may be the one that delivery
  just cleared. The park waits for a post-delivery listing, for at most 30 s.

sites, site bot announced COMPLETE) and 09:22:08 `S01E03.DV` (parked 2.3 s after its
