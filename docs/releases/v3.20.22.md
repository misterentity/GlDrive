# GL Drive v3.20.22

Route priority for spreading can now follow a fixed chain.

A race already relays through sites as they receive files: any site that holds
a file can send it on, and each route's share of the transfer score comes from
the average speed of its last ten transfers. That remains the default.

**Settings → Spread → "Use a fixed route chain instead of measured route
speed"** replaces the speed share with an order you set, for example
`sitea, siteb, sitec`:

- the next hop in the chain (sitea → siteb, siteb → sitec) ranks highest;
- skipping ahead (sitea → sitec) ranks lower the further it skips;
- going backwards, or a site not in the chain, earns nothing from the chain.

The chain ranks routes and never forbids one, so a race still completes when a
hop is down or out of slots. SFV files still go first, and never-source and
download-only sites keep their roles. Sites may be written by name or id,
separated by commas or `>`. The keys are `spread.useRouteChain` and
`spread.routeChain`.
