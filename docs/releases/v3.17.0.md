# GL Drive v3.17.0

Feature-gap roadmap, Phase C batch 2 (C6–C10), which completes Phase C. Codex implemented it, and three independent reviewers plus Claude reviewed it. Full suite: 4,825 tests (4,752 app, 53 core, 20 MCP), 0 failures.

## Why is it waiting? (C6)

- Queued races, parked downloads and offline sites show:
  - a reason code;
  - the limiting constraint (login budget, account hold, site-health retry, transfer gate, queue position);
  - the next check, and an ETA where one is knowable.
- The view is read-only and adds no polling or FTP traffic.
- There is deliberately no "Check now" that could bypass the login budget, BNC or authentication holds, or the site-health ladder.
- Starting a race through the REST API now returns 202 when the race is queued and 409 when it is suppressed.

## IRC mentions, PMs and log search (C7)

- Your nick in channel chat and private messages raises a local tray notice and an unread badge. Notices stay on the PC, never go to push targets, and are limited to one per channel or conversation per 10 minutes, with a count.
- Lines from configured sitebots, lines matched as announces, and announce-only channels are never highlights.
- Badges count mentions and PMs, not ordinary channel traffic.
- **Search** (Ctrl+F in the IRC tab): regex over the last 30 days of channel logs and, optionally, PM history.
  - The search runs in the background, with a 25 ms per-match timeout and a 2 s overall budget.
  - It keeps the newest 200 results.
  - PM history stays DPAPI-protected at rest. Search patterns and results never reach MCP, the AI agent or any push target.

## Saved feed views (C8)

- Named filters on the release feed (text, category, server), each with an unread count.
- "Mute in feed" only hides rows from that view. It never changes monitoring, the wishlist, auto-race or alerts.

## Bulk actions with Undo (C9)

- Multi-select in the wishlist, release feed, downloads and race queue.
- Removals are applied 10 seconds later with **Undo**; closing the dashboard cancels them.
- When a removal applies, it re-checks current state:
  - removing download history never deletes files;
  - race-queue removal skips starting, active, owned-partial and cleanup entries.
- Bulk Race or Download from the feed asks for confirmation above one row, is capped at 25 rows, and saves once.

## Collection gaps (C10)

- For a wishlist or owned movie, an explicit **Collection** refresh lists the rest of its TMDB collection and marks each film as owned, missing or unknown.
  - It uses the existing library index and downloads; it never scans or polls on its own.
  - It's bounded: two requests, a 30-second deadline and a 2 MiB response cap.
- **Add** puts a known-missing film on the wishlist through the normal add path, with your default quality and the CAM floor intact. Undated films and films whose ownership is unknown aren't added.

## Review

The reviewers found 13 issues, all fixed before release. The ones that would have affected default users:

- **Bulk race-queue removal could act on rows you had deselected.** The list kept selection inside each row's equality, so WPF lost track of deselected rows. "Remove selected" would then have dropped races you no longer had selected. Selection is now tracked by request id.
- **Mention and PM notices had no throttle.** Any line containing your nick raised its own tray balloon, sitebot output included.
- **Bulk feed actions had no cap or confirmation**, and they rewrote a whole store once per row on the UI thread.
- **Every IRC line recomputed all unread counts on the UI thread.** Updates are now incremental.
- Download waiting reasons went stale after a retry, and a null nick in config threw on every message.
