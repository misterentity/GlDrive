## Findings and changes

Review of installed v3.20.10 covered 24 retained log chunks,
**12:29:20.754–16:03:36.741 UTC**: 1,596,890 records, one Warning and no Error/Fatal
entries. Severity totals alone concealed failures: four Information-level download
failures occurred at 13:37:40.936–14:13:50.170 UTC. Incident capsules preserve
disk-full extraction errors at November 4 21:10:12.529 and November 5 10:38:58.520
UTC; the latter belongs to the v3.20.10 process lifetime. Retained successful
activity includes 621 FXP transfers and 27 completed races.

- **Extraction lacked an unpacked-output reservation.** It now reserves admitted
  archive entries before writing and preserves archives on disk-full failure.
  Atomic output-length validation remains enforced. A durable verified-input
  checkpoint parks extraction without consuming retries. Valid recovery proceeds locally despite FTP cooldown or race
  priority; changed inputs require normal verification. Recovery stays within the
  download root and rejects reparse paths.
- **Capacity admission marked recoverable shortages Failed.** Work now parks with
  a persisted identity-bound hint and remeasures local files without repeated source
  calls while denied. Restored capacity requires a fresh source plan. Explicit
  Pause/Resume or Cancel/Retry clears the hint. Single-file plans previously supplied
  `Size=-1`, making reservation ineffective; they now resolve SIZE before admission,
  with exact parent-listing fallback. Unknown directory-entry sizes also resolve.
- **Relocation omitted configured search roots.** Recovery now checks exact category
  and flat-release paths beneath those roots, covering the `/recent` to `/incoming`
  misses established in the earlier production observation. Validation, deduplication
  and lookup budgets remain; no recursive crawl was added.
- **Torrent completion preceded durable checkpoint completion.** Dedicated queued
  engines now use Accurate resume mode, pause on the library loop and await completion
  before Stop/Remove. A resume-write failure after Stopped still unregisters the
  manager and propagates failure. Player streaming keeps its existing policy.

Two retained local writes took 54 and 43 seconds. Existing handling correctly treats
disk waits separately from network stalls. System-event review found no matching
disk/controller/NTFS hardware errors; available evidence does not establish a
specific hardware fault. Capacity fixes cannot guarantee storage throughput.

The local deployment monitor now rejects explicit failed download/extraction/race outcomes
and incomplete index builds at every severity. Fixtures and the historical
Information-level failures validate that gate. SITEC's documented remote FTP outage
is counted separately; other warnings require review.

## Remaining risks

- Physical capacity, storage latency and external FTP/metadata availability remain
  outside these fixes. Other processes can consume space after admission.
- Verbose logging remains configured. The bounded 24-file cap retained only about
  3.6 hours under heavy listing traffic; census/capsule samples report drops and
  cannot establish exhaustive overnight counts.
- Unresolved FTP sizes fail before writing. While parked, upstream inventory changes
  are discovered after capacity returns or explicit Pause/Resume; local cache changes
  are remeasured automatically.
- Accurate torrent resume may rehash after abrupt termination. A 60-second missing
  completion notification fails with owned cleanup; timeout alone does not prove
  a stalled dependency checkpoint drained.
- Standing channel/index warnings and frozen decisions remain. Credentials, TLS,
  routing and user destinations were not changed. Bounded smoke cannot establish
  long-term uptime; live rollback and alternate-administrator installation were not tested.

Automatic approval review rejected optional cleanup of the first failed native
fixture with **“blocked by policy.”** It was not retried or bypassed. Its stopped,
isolated synthetic data remains; final-run cleanup succeeded normally.
