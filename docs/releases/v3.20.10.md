## Cleanup: extraction engine and Upcoming tab

- The archive extraction engine moved out of the Extractor window into `Downloads/ArchiveExtraction` (no WPF). Startup auto-watch, the Extractor window and the MCP `extractor_*` tools share one engine; automation no longer opens a hidden window. Settings and abandon records keep the same files and format.
- The Extractor window opened from the tray now shows in the taskbar.
- The dashboard's Upcoming tab has its own view model. MCP UI automation addresses its commands as `Upcoming.LoadUpcomingCommand` etc.
